BEFORE YOU PULL UP A CHAIR

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Is this for complete beginners?

Yes. First Table Coach starts with the goal of American Mahjong and the tiles themselves. You can follow the guided path and return to lessons whenever you need a refresher.

Is this a Mahjong game I can play online?

It’s a learning companion with guided lessons and interactive exercises, including a scripted first hand. It does not offer online multiplayer games.

Does the app include an official current-year card?

The app uses a practice card for teaching. Bring your own current-year card for actual play. First Table Coach is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League.

How does the full course unlock?

A one-time in-app purchase unlocks all nine modules, with no recurring subscription. See the app for the current price.
