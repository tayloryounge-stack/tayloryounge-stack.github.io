您的训练打卡记录、训练目标与难度设置、每周训练日安排、器械偏好、提醒时间，以及身体围度数据（体重、体脂率、腰围）和语言偏好，均保存在当前设备的本地存储中，用于生成训练计划、统计进度、绘制围度趋势图和展示历史日历。身体围度数据仅保存在本机，不会上传至云端。

LeanClub · Home Lean Workout
Privacy Policy

LeanClub is a personal home bodyweight and dumbbell workout planner, exercise guide, and body measurement tracker provided by FANG SHAO. This policy explains how the current version handles information.

1. Local records and their purpose

Your workout logs, training goals, difficulty level, weekly schedule, equipment preferences, reminder time, language settings, and body measurements (weight, body fat percentage, and waist circumference) are stored locally on your current device. They are used to generate workout plans, calculate progress statistics, render body metric trend charts, and display your workout history calendar. Body measurement values stay on your device and are not uploaded to cloud storage.

2. Accounts, sync, and usage analytics

This version requires no account registration and provides no automatic cloud sync for workout logs or body measurements. We use Amplitude to analyze feature usage, workout sessions, and purchase flows to improve the app, including screen views, language selection, onboarding completion, workout starts and completions, product loading, purchases, and restore outcomes. Sent data includes a randomly generated app user identifier stored on your device, an SDK-generated device identifier, events and timestamps, sessions, app and OS versions, device model, language, country or region, training goals and settings, workout duration and progress, and boolean flags indicating whether weight, body fat, or waist entries were recorded (actual measurement values are never sent). The same random user identifier is used by RevenueCat to associate your LeanClub Pro entitlements. Network connections may involve IP addresses and approximate location derived from them. Data is used solely for this app’s analytics and service operation, never for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Apple purchases and Pro access

Monthly subscriptions, annual subscriptions, and lifetime purchases are billed by the Apple App Store. RevenueCat is used to load products, verify transactions, manage subscription status, and restore Pro access. RevenueCat receives the random app user identifier, product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration dates, refund or revocation status, storefront, and device/app metadata required to operate the service. We do not receive or store payment card details or Apple Account passwords, nor do we upload your workout logs or body measurements to the billing service. Analytics and purchase records are retained as needed to provide these services under the providers’ policies and applicable law.

4. JSON backups and system clipboard

Only when you explicitly tap "Export Backup" does the app serialize your local settings, workout history, and body measurements as JSON text into the system clipboard so you can paste and save it yourself. Only when you explicitly tap "Restore Data" and confirm does the app read JSON text from the clipboard to restore your records. Exporting does not create an automatic cloud backup; any third-party app where you paste your backup processes data under its own policies. Please keep exported backup copies secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you use the in-app "Erase All Data" control or uninstall the app. System backups depend on your device settings and Apple services; uninstalling does not automatically remove system backups or JSON backup copies you have saved elsewhere. To inquire about or request access or deletion of analytics or purchase data associated with your identifier, email us below; we may need to verify the association between your request and the relevant identifiers.

6. Support requests

When you email us, we receive your email address, message content, and any attachments you choose to provide to handle technical support and privacy inquiries. Please avoid sending full personal backup files, and redact unnecessary personal information from screenshots. Support emails are retained as needed to resolve the request and then deleted or anonymized unless retention is legally required.

7. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your local workout logs or body measurements or use them for advertising profiles. Apple, RevenueCat, and Amplitude process necessary technical and transaction information under their own privacy policies to provide in-app purchases, entitlement verification, and product analytics, which may involve processing in other countries or regions where they operate. We may also respond to lawful information requests.

8. Contact and policy updates

For support, privacy questions, or requests regarding your information, contact support@funnyday.xyz and describe the relevant feature and issue. If our data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date and provide an in-app notice where appropriate.
