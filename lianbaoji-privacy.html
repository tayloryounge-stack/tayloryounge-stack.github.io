Privacy Policy · English

Overview

LeanClub provides home workout plans, exercise guidance and tracking. This policy explains how the app and its service providers process information. Contact: support@funnyday.xyz.

Data on your device

Workout records, training preferences and body measurements such as weight, body fat and waist are stored on your device. The backup feature copies data to the clipboard for you to save or paste back. Protect any copies you make.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand app usage, workouts and purchase flows and improve the product. Information includes a randomly generated app user ID, device or SDK identifiers, app and operating system versions, device model, language, country or region, event times, training goals and settings, workout duration and progress, and whether weight, body fat or waist entries were provided. Network connections may involve IP addresses and approximate location derived from them. Current app events do not include the actual weight, body fat or waist values.

Purchases

Apple App Store processes purchases. When enabled, RevenueCat retrieves products, verifies purchases and restores access; it may process purchase and subscription status, product and transaction identifiers, app user or device identifiers, and device information needed for the service. Payment providers handle card details.

Retention and choices

The app provides controls to delete records and clear data. Backups may remain on your clipboard or wherever you saved them. Analytics and purchase services may retain processed information as needed to operate, under their policies and applicable law. For access or deletion requests, contact support@funnyday.xyz; we may need to verify the connection between a request and the data.

Service providers
