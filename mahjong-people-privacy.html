麻将人 - 牌局记分
隐私政策

麻将人是一款用于线下麻将的手动记分与个人记录工具，由 FANG SHAO 提供。本政策说明当前版本如何处理信息。

1. 本机记录与用途

您输入的日期、场地、玩家姓名或昵称、座位、玩法、分数、牌友备注，以及语言和偏好设置，保存在当前设备的本地数据库中，用于记分、历史查询、统计和牌桌管理。场地是您手动填写的文字，不通过设备定位获取。请只填写必要信息，并尽量使用昵称。

2. 账号、同步和分析

当前版本无需注册账号，不提供应用云同步。应用没有接入广告、跨应用追踪或第三方行为分析 SDK，也不会将本机牌局、牌友和分数记录上传至每日牌运服务。

3. 每日牌运与联网服务

打开每日牌运时，应用会向 mahjong-course.funnyday.xyz 请求所选日期及语言对应的内容，并从该域名加载图片。请求参数包含日期、天数及所选语言。完成网络请求时，服务及其网络提供方会接收到 IP 地址、请求时间、请求路径等必要的连接信息；这些信息可能被用于内容交付、网络安全和故障处理。该服务使用 Cloudflare Workers、R2 和网络基础设施。每日牌运仅供娱乐。

4. 访问日志

我们不建立或保留每日牌运接口和图片服务的访问日志，不将 IP 地址或请求时间保存为用户访问记录。网络提供方仍需处理完成连接所必需的信息，并可能为其自身的基础设施安全、反滥用或法定义务按其政策处理必要数据。这不表示联网过程中不会处理任何数据。

5. Apple 内购与会员权益

年订阅和永久解锁通过 Apple App Store 处理付款。应用使用 StoreKit 在设备上验证 Apple 签名的交易，并读取商品标识、购买或到期时间、撤销状态等信息，以判断会员权益和恢复购买。我们不接收银行卡或支付密码，不建立自有购买账号，也不将牌局记录上传至购买服务。会员分析和年度报告在设备本地计算；仅在您选择复制报告时写入系统剪贴板。牌桌主题偏好保存在设备上。Apple 按其隐私政策处理购买与账户信息。

6. CSV 与剪贴板

仅在您主动选择 CSV 导出时，应用才将牌局日期、场地、座位、分数和玩法写入系统剪贴板，供您粘贴至表格或自行保存。导出不会自动生成云备份。粘贴到其他应用后的处理受其自身政策约束。

7. 保存期限与删除

本机记录会保留至您通过应用删除相关内容，或通过系统移除应用数据。删除牌友或场地资料不会自动删除已有历史牌局中保存的文字。系统备份由您的设备设置及 Apple 服务决定，卸载不保证删除系统备份或您已导出的副本；这些副本需要您单独管理。网络服务方按其服务政策及账户日志设置处理必要连接信息。您可通过下方邮箱询问适用的日志保留设置，并提出与您相关的访问、更正或删除请求。

8. 支持请求

您发邮件时，我们会收到您的邮箱、邮件内容及您主动提供的附件，用于处理咨询、故障和隐私请求。请避免发送真实牌友资料或完整牌局数据库；截图请先遮盖不必要的个人信息。支持邮件按处理事项所需的期限保存，处理结束后删除或匿名化，依法需要保留的除外。

9. 服务提供方与信息共享

我们不出售本机记录，不将其用于广告画像。Cloudflare 会为提供网络服务而处理必要的连接信息；您主动使用的系统备份、邮件和其他服务按各自政策处理信息。服务提供方可能在其运营的其他国家或地区处理相关网络信息。我们也可能依法响应合法的信息请求。

如需咨询、反馈或行使与您信息相关的权利，请联系 support@funnyday.xyz。请说明所涉功能和问题，我们可能需要核实请求与您的关联。信息处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页及生效日期；如有需要，也会通过应用提供提示。

Mahjong People - Scorekeeping
Privacy Policy

Mahjong People is a manual scorekeeping and personal recordkeeping app for in-person Mahjong, provided by FANG SHAO. This policy describes the current version.

1. Local records and their purpose

Dates, venues, player names or nicknames, seats, game styles, scores, player notes, language and preferences are stored in a local database on your current device for scorekeeping, history, statistics and table management. Venues are text you enter, not device location data. Enter only necessary information and use nicknames where possible.

2. Accounts, sync and analytics

This version requires no account and provides no app cloud sync. The app does not integrate advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. Local games, player records and scores are not sent to the daily fortune service.

3. Daily fortune and network services

Opening daily fortune requests content for the selected date and language from mahjong-course.funnyday.xyz and loads its images from the same domain. Parameters contain the date, number of days and selected language. The service and network providers receive connection information such as your IP address, request time and path, which may be used for content delivery, security and troubleshooting. The service uses Cloudflare Workers, R2 and network infrastructure. Fortune content is for entertainment only.

4. Access logs

We do not create or retain access logs for the daily fortune API or image service, or store IP addresses or request times as user access records. Network providers still process information necessary to establish connections and may process necessary data under their own policies for infrastructure security, abuse prevention or legal obligations. This does not mean that no data is processed during network access.

5. Apple purchases and Plus access

Annual subscriptions and lifetime purchases are billed by the Apple App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify Apple-signed transactions on-device and reads product identifiers, purchase or expiration dates and revocation status to determine access and restore purchases. We do not receive card details or payment passwords, create a separate purchase account, or upload game records to a billing service. Plus analysis and annual reports are calculated locally; reports enter the system clipboard only when you choose to copy them. Table theme preferences are saved on-device. Apple handles purchase and account information under its privacy policy.

6. CSV and clipboard

Only when you choose CSV export does the app copy game dates, venues, seats, scores and styles to the system clipboard for you to paste or save. This does not create a cloud backup. Other apps handle pasted information under their own policies.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete the relevant entries in the app or remove app data through your operating system. Deleting a player or venue does not automatically erase its text from historical games. System backups depend on device settings and Apple services. Uninstalling does not guarantee deletion of backups or exported copies; manage these separately. Network providers process necessary connection information under their service policies and account logging settings. Contact us below to ask about applicable log retention settings or request access, correction or deletion of information relating to you.

8. Support requests

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to provide, to handle support and privacy requests. Avoid sending real player details or your full database, and redact unnecessary personal information from screenshots. Support messages are retained as needed to handle the matter, then deleted or anonymized, unless retention is legally required.

9. Providers and sharing

We do not sell local records or use them for advertising profiles. Cloudflare processes necessary connection information to provide network services. System backup, email and other services you choose follow their own policies. Providers may process network information in other countries or regions where they operate. We may also respond to lawful information requests.

10. Contact and updates

For support, privacy questions or requests concerning your information, email support@funnyday.xyz and describe the relevant feature and issue. We may need to verify the request relates to you. If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date and provide an in-app notice where appropriate.
